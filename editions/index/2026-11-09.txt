Afronomics African Sovereign Bill Index — week of 9 November 2026

The Afronomics African Sovereign Bill Index (ASBI) stands at 12.69% for the week of 9 November 2026, up 4 basis points from the previous week, +32 bps on the month and −182 bps on the year.

Week on week, Uganda rose 25 bps to 14.75%, Egypt rose 16 bps to 26.08%; the other 8 markets had no new one-year result.

The index is the equal-weighted average of the latest 364-day Treasury bill rate in 10 markets. Egypt pays most (26.08%) and Tanzania least (6.13%). The series high is 19.82% (11 March 2024) and the low 9.27% (27 July 2020).
