## Analysis

The figures above are as CBK and KDIC published them; the three recovery percentages are not on the same basis and should not be read as a trend without reconciling the definitions. In the CBK record, the failure came to light through the board's own disclosure, not through a published supervisory signal; what a reader could have seen beforehand is still open (see TODO).
