## 1. Definitions

- **Data**: the datasets, files, feeds, tables and API responses Afronomics makes available to the Customer under the
  Order Form, including updates, in any format.
- **Source Data**: figures published by a third party (for example a central bank, treasury, debt office or
  statistics office) that Afronomics collects and includes in the Data.
- **Derived Output**: analysis, charts, models, reports, indices or figures the Customer creates using the Data, in
  which the Data cannot reasonably be extracted or reconstructed as a substitute for the Data.
- **Authorised Users**: the named individuals or teams employed or engaged by the Customer set out in the Order Form.
- **Key**: an API or download credential issued to the Customer.
- **Permitted Purpose**: the Customer's internal business purposes, including analysis, models, investment committee
  papers, risk management and advice to its own clients, as limited by the Order Form.
- **Third Party**: anyone other than the Customer and its Authorised Users, including the Customer's clients and
  affiliates unless named in the Order Form.

non-sublicensable licence, for the Term and the territory stated in the Order Form (worldwide if none), for its
Authorised Users to access, download, store and use the Data for the Permitted Purpose.

2.2 The Customer may publish or give to Third Parties **Derived Output** and **insubstantial extracts** of the Data
(individual figures or small tables in reports, presentations and client materials) provided each is credited
"Source: Afronomics, from [publisher]" and the extract cannot reasonably serve as a substitute for the Data.

## 3. Restrictions

The Customer must not, and must ensure its Authorised Users do not, except as expressly allowed by this Agreement:
(a) redistribute, sell, sublicense, rent, publish or otherwise make the Data available to any Third Party;
(b) use the Data to create a product or service that competes with Afronomics, including a database of auction results;
(c) use the Data to train, fine-tune or evaluate machine-learning models made available to Third Parties;
(d) remove or alter attributions, source links or notices;
(e) share Keys outside the Authorised Users, or use more Keys or seats than ordered;
(f) circumvent rate limits, access controls or technical measures, or scrape the website to obtain Data outside the
Key; or
(g) use the Data in breach of law, including sanctions law.

## 4. Source Data and third-party rights

4.1 The Data is compiled from Source Data published by third parties, each row linked to its source where available.
Source Data remains subject to its publisher's rights and terms. Afronomics grants rights only in what it is entitled to
license; it does not license any third party's text, logos or documents.

4.2 Afronomics may remove any dataset or field from the Data if a publisher withdraws permission, changes its terms or
objects, or if continued supply would breach law. If this materially reduces the Data ordered, the Customer may
terminate the affected Order Form and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

4.3 **Excluded data.** Unless the Order Form says otherwise, the Data does not include foreign-exchange reference rates,
third-party headlines or excerpts, Eurobond secondary prices, or any data Afronomics obtained from a commercial
vendor. [Advocate: confirm list against `legal-review-2026-10.md` §4.]

faith to verify it. Underpaid fees found are payable at list price.

## 12. Term and termination

12.1 This Agreement starts on the Start Date in the Order Form and continues for the Term. Unless the Order Form says
otherwise, monthly Order Forms renew for successive months only on payment of the next month's fee; annual Order Forms
renew only by a new Order Form.

12.2 Either party may terminate by written notice if the other materially breaches this Agreement and (if remediable)
does not remedy it within 14 days of notice, or becomes insolvent or enters any insolvency process.

12.3 Afronomics may suspend access immediately where needed to stop a breach of clause 3 or to comply with law.

12.4 On termination or expiry: the licence ends; Keys are revoked [3] days after the end of the paid period; the Customer
stops using the Data and deletes it within 30 days, except (a) Derived Output already created, (b) copies it must keep

documents already published under clause 2.2.

12.5 Clauses 3, 5, 6 (for accrued fees), 7.2-7.3, 8, 9, 12.4 and 14 survive.
