## Who is responsible

- **Administrator:** Afronomics, which publishes, computes and licenses both indices.
- **Method owner:** the founder. Only the founder can approve a change to a method, a new version, a suspension or
  a cessation. Each approval is logged in the decision log and in that index's revision log.
- **Calculation:** automated code reading the data files named in each method. No reading is entered or adjusted
  by hand. A person may only fix an input (with its source) or the code, and the fix is logged.

## The oversight panel

Both indices stay labelled **indicative reference rates** until an oversight panel is appointed.

**Role.** The panel checks that each index is computed as its method says, reviews every method change before it is
announced, reviews the revision logs, corrections and complaints at least twice a year, and signs off the annual
review. It can ask for any data, code or log. It does not set or approve readings.

**Make-up.** Three members, at least two of them independent of Afronomics: for example, someone who has worked in
a central bank's markets or statistics function, someone from a newsroom or research body that uses the figures, and
a person with risk or audit experience. None may be paid by a licensee to influence the indices.

**Terms.** Two years, renewable once. Members serve unpaid or for a fixed fee that does not depend on any reading
or on licence income. The panel meets at least twice a year, and publishes a short note after each meeting on the
method page.

## Conflicts of interest

- Afronomics takes no payment to include, exclude, weight or describe any market, lender or product in an index,
  and no institution can pay to be rated.
- No government, central bank, lender or licensee sees a reading before it is published, or has a say in a method.
- Everyone who can change a method, the code or the data, and every panel member, declares once a year and whenever
  it changes: holdings in, or trading of, instruments whose price an index follows (for the ASBI, Treasury bills of
  a covered market); paid work for a covered government, central bank, bank, SACCO or lender; and any other interest
  in a reading. Declarations are listed below.
- Someone with a conflict on a question steps out of the decision on it, and that is logged.

## Records

Each index keeps its method, its revision log and its published releases in version control, so every published
value and every change to the method can be traced to a date and a reason.
