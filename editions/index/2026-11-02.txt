Afronomics African Sovereign Bill Index — week of 2 November 2026

The Afronomics African Sovereign Bill Index (ASBI) stands at 12.65% for the week of 2 November 2026, up 1 basis points from the previous week, +27 bps on the month and −186 bps on the year.

Week on week, Egypt rose 21 bps to 25.91%, Ghana fell 7 bps to 9.60%, Nigeria fell 5 bps to 15.80%; the other 7 markets had no new one-year result.

The index is the equal-weighted average of the latest 364-day Treasury bill rate in 10 markets. Egypt pays most (25.91%) and Tanzania least (6.13%). The series high is 19.82% (11 March 2024) and the low 9.27% (27 July 2020).
