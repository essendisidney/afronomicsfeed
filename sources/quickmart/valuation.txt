    Stanbic Bank Kenya Limited and SBG Securities (together “Stanbic”), are pleased to submit this Offer Pricing Methodology document for Quick Mart PLC (“Quickmart”
    or the “Company”), to the Board of Directors (“Directors”), for the purposes of fulfilling the Capital Market Authority’s (“CMA”) listing requirements in relation to the
    proposed initial public offering (“IPO”) of the Company’s ordinary shares on the Main Investment Market Segment (“MIMS”) of the Nairobi Securities Exchange (“NSE”).
    Quickmart is 100% owned by Sokoni Retail Kenya Limited (“SRK” or the “Selling Shareholder”).                                    
                                                                                                                                    
    This document sets out the Offer Price contained in the Information Memorandum (the “IM”) and summarises the market multiples relevant to the Offer Price. The fixed
    Offer Price of KES 7.50 per Ordinary Share (“Offer Price)” was approved by the Selling Shareholder and the Directors via a resolution dated 30 September
    2026. Based on the Offer Price of KES 7.50 per share and the Company’s 4.0 billion issued ordinary shares, Quickmart has an implied equity value (market
    capitalisation) of KES 30.0 billion. The Offer Price reflects a discount to the assessed equity value of the Company, as determined by the Selling Shareholder, taking
    into account prevailing market conditions, investor feedback received during the pre-IPO process, due diligence findings and the final structure, timing and size of the
    Offer.                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                    
    Stanbic has relied upon information provided by or on behalf of the Company, including management’s financial projections and assumptions, as well as relevant market
    data. Stanbic has not independently verified its accuracy, completeness or reliability. No separate due diligence was undertaken for the purposes of preparing this
    analysis.                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                    
    This document has been prepared solely for the purpose described above and should not be regarded as an independent valuation, fairness opinion, investment
    recommendation or investment advice. It should therefore be read in conjunction with the disclaimers and the guiding principles contained therein.
                                                                                                                                    
    Potential investors will be encouraged to seek independent professional advice from their stockbroker, investment adviser, banker, financial adviser, legal adviser or
    other qualified advisers before making an investment decision.
