## Analysis

In CBK's account, the immediate cause was a liquidity run (social media reports and two directors stepping aside) rather than a capital shortfall announced beforehand; CBK's later references to recovering funds "obtained irregularly" and assets "illegally acquired" point to problems inside the bank, but no figure for them was read in this task.
