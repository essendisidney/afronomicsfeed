A shopper in Nyeri who wants a piece of Quickmart has until **5:00 pm today**. After that, the offer of 2,000,000,000 shares at **KES 7.50** each is closed.

Quickmart's offer announcement sets the rules. The smallest application is **500 shares**, and above that, applications go up in steps of 100 shares. Once an application is in, it cannot be withdrawn.

## The rules, as the offer announcement sets them

| Term | What the announcement says |
| --- | --- |
| Shares on offer | 2,000,000,000 existing shares, half the company |
| Price | KES 7.50 a share; KES 15 billion in all |
| Smallest application | 500 shares, then multiples of 100 shares |
| Largest application | No maximum |
| Closes | 5:00 pm (EAT), 30 October 2026; also the last day of payment for retail investors |
| Minimum take-up | 75% of the shares (1,500,000,000), or the offer does not proceed |
| Underwritten | No |
| Withdrawals | Not allowed without the company's written consent |

*Source: Quick Mart PLC, public announcement of the offer, 5 October 2026.*

**Facts.** You can apply online at the offer portal or by dialling the USSD code \*483\*803#. The USSD route is only for applications worth up to KES 250,000. Paper forms go to the placing agents by 5:00 pm today. Every applicant needs a CDS account, the account at the Central Depository where listed shares are held.

**Facts.** The shares are existing shares, sold by Sokoni Retail Kenya, the selling shareholder: an offer for sale, not new shares issued by the company. The Capital Markets Authority approved the offer; the announcement says that approval "is not to be taken as an indication of the merits" of the company or the shares.

## What happens next

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 6 November 2026 | Results announced; applicants told their allocation |
| 11 November 2026 | Shares credited to CDS accounts; refunds, if any, begin |
| 12 November 2026 | Shares start trading on the Nairobi Securities Exchange |

**Facts.** If buyers apply for less than 75% of the shares by today's close, the offer does not go ahead and all application money is refunded **without interest**.

## What it means

**For your money** (Analysis): money paid today is committed until the results on 6 November at the earliest, and any refund only begins on 11 November. Because applications are irrevocable, there is no stepping back in between.

**For the size of your application** (Analysis): the steps of 100 shares mean not every amount buys a whole application. Work out the number of shares first, then the amount.

## What to check before 5pm

- That your CDS account details are correct, because shares cannot be credited without one.
- That your application is 500 shares or more, in multiples of 100.
- The information memorandum dated 2 October 2026, published at the official offer site, which the announcement tells applicants to read in full.
