## A. Nigeria savings bond closes Friday

Nigeria's October savings bond is open until Friday 9 October.

From the Debt Management Office's offer:
• 2-year bond: 13.071% a year, due 14 Oct 2028
• 3-year bond: 14.071% a year, due 14 Oct 2029
• From ₦5,000, in units of ₦1,000, up to ₦50 million

## B. What a mobile loan really costs

Borrow KES 1,000 on your phone for 30 days. What does it cost, at the prices each provider publishes?

• M-Shwari: KES 90 (9% for one month)
• Tala: from KES 90 (from 0.3% a day)
• Fuliza: KES 190 (1% access fee, plus KES 6 a day on a KES 1,000 balance)

As a simple yearly rate, that is about 110% for M-Shwari, from 110% for Tala, and 231% for Fuliza.

The average bank lending rate is 14.34% a year (Central Bank of Kenya).

Mobile credit is fast and it can save a day. Kept for a month, it costs many times a bank loan. Repay early where you can.

Each charge is quoted from the provider's own page, with the link:
