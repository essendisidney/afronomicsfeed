# Treasury Desk sales kit

28 October 2026. This kit is for the 90-day test in `docs/strategy/founder-strategy-pack.md` section K. It operates under D-035 (the Treasury Desk is the lead product) and D-036 (outreach is allowed only to the 60 names in `treasury-desk-targets.md`).

The founder does all the contacting, using LinkedIn from the founder's own account, phone, introductions and meetings in person. Outgoing email is still on hold (D-030, D-033). The desk prepares drafts and sample packs and sends nothing.

Two rules hold throughout:
- **Facts only.** We report what the market did. We never recommend what a client should buy, never rate or rank a named institution (D-028), and never take commission from anyone we write about.
- **Every sales document carries the disclaimer** in section 8.

> **Your board's rates page, done.**
>
> **What you get**
> - **On the first working day of each month:** four sourced A4 pages with your institution's name on the cover. > - **Each auction day:** an alert the day the Central Bank posts the result, with the change on the week.

## 3. Demo script (20 minutes, in person or on a call)

1. **Their world (4 minutes).** Ask, then listen. Write the answers into the desk log.
   - "When does the board or investment committee next meet?"
   - "Who prepares the rates and investments page today? How long does it take?"
   - "Where does it come from: CBK, your fund manager's report, a bank?"
   - "Has the board ever asked whether you are getting the market rate?"
2. **The sample with their name on the cover (6 minutes).** Open `/pack/sample?for=<Institution>`.
   - **Page 1:** this month's auctions. Click a figure's source and show the CBK PDF opening.
   - **Page 2:** the curve. "Bills pay almost the same at three months and a year. The term premium is in bonds of 15 years and longer." Present this as a description of the market, not advice.
   - **Page 3:** the funds' published yields, alphabetical, each with its source and date.
   - **Page 4:** the calendar and the region.
3. **The benchmark (4 minutes; Treasury Desk prospects only).** Open the sample benchmark page at `/benchmarking/sample`. "You send holdings; we show what the same shillings earned elsewhere, in shillings. Facts only; your adviser and your board decide."
4. **Independence and corrections (2 minutes).** Show `/trust` and `/corrections`. "We are not paid by any fund or bank on that page."
5. **The close (4 minutes).**
   - "Shall I send next month's pack, with your name on it, before your board meets on [date]?" This is the free sample.
   - Then: "If the board finds it useful, the founding-member place is KES 12,000 a month for two years, invoiced once a year. There are ten places."
   - Log the outcome: next board date, objection, price quoted and decision-maker.

**Never say:**
- "you should buy";
- "the best fund";
- "we recommend";
- "guaranteed".

**If asked "what would you do?":** "We report the market; for a recommendation, your licensed adviser is the right person, and the pack gives them the same facts."

## 4. Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Our fund manager sends us this free." | "They do, and you should keep reading it. The difference is that they manage the money the report is about. Ours is from no one with a product to sell, and every figure is sourced. Many boards like to see both." |
| "We can get it from CBK ourselves." | "You can, and it's where ours comes from. The pack saves the day of copying, puts the auctions, curve, funds and shilling on one page, and shows the source for every figure, so the board can check it." |
| "Send it and we'll see." | "I'll send a sample with your name on it for the [date] meeting. What would the board need to see in it to say yes?" |
| "We need board approval to buy anything." | "Then let's time it to the meeting. I'll send the sample a week before, plus a one-paragraph proposal the treasurer can table." Use the pilot proposal in section 5. |
| "Too expensive." | "It's less than a day of an analyst's time each month. The pack-only version is KES 12,000. A founding-member place locks that for two years." |
| "Is this investment advice? Are you licensed?" | "No. It's published market information: what the auctions and funds did, with sources. We don't recommend, we don't rate institutions and we take no commissions. The disclaimer is on every page." |

> - An alert on each auction day.
> - For the Treasury Desk tier, one quarterly benchmark page.
>
> **Price.** KES [12,000 / 18,000] a month, invoiced for 3 months (KES [36,000 / 54,000]). It converts to a 12-month subscription at the founding-member price only if the board agrees at the end of the pilot. Otherwise it ends with no further charge.
>
> **Success measure.** The board uses the pack at at least two of the three meetings, and the treasurer spends no more than an hour a month on the rates page.
>
> **Independence.** Afronomics Feed does not manage money, sell investment products or receive commissions from any institution in the pack.
>
> *[Disclaimer, section 8]*

The pilot is **paid**, because a free pilot does not test willingness to pay. A free *sample* (one month) is fine before the pilot.
