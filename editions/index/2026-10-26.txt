Afronomics African Sovereign Bill Index — week of 26 October 2026

The Afronomics African Sovereign Bill Index (ASBI) stands at 12.65% for the week of 26 October 2026, up 28 basis points from the previous week, +25 bps on the month and −175 bps on the year.

Week on week, Uganda rose 300 bps to 14.50%, Tanzania fell 15 bps to 6.13%, Ghana fell 4 bps to 9.67%, Egypt rose 3 bps to 25.70%; the other 6 markets had no new one-year result.

The index is the equal-weighted average of the latest 364-day Treasury bill rate in 10 markets. Egypt pays most (25.70%) and Tanzania least (6.13%). The series high is 19.82% (11 March 2024) and the low 9.27% (27 July 2020).
